Our Story

A Little Café with a Big Heart

The Cozy Cup started as a dream — a place where the aroma of freshly ground coffee fills the air and every corner invites you to slow down. We source our beans from local farms and bake everything fresh each morning.

Whether you're catching up with a friend or finding your quiet corner to work, we've got the perfect cup waiting for you.
